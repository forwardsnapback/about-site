02 // work

The day job

What I do when someone's paying me to.

I work for a managed service provider (MSP). I like untangling networks and making small and medium-sized businesses more secure. Here's what I do on my quest to leave things better than I found them.

A-01

Entra ID

Users, groups, and access in Microsoft Entra ID, plus the conditional access policies that decide who gets in.

A-02

M365 migrations

I move email, files, and Teams over to Microsoft 365. I've migrated hundreds of mailboxes and users from on-premises servers to the cloud.

A-03

Networking

My favorite job: tearing out an old, flaky network and rebuilding it right. Day to day that means UniFi, Meraki, and Fortinet, with VLANs to keep traffic separated.

A-04

Security hardening

Defense in layers, built together with each client: we agree on the policies up front, and nothing changes without their sign-off. I've brought every one of our clients onto MFA and set up patching schedules in N-able RMM across their networks.

A-05

SharePoint

I migrate users into SharePoint, then use Power Automate to take repetitive tasks off their plate and SharePoint lists to help teams take control of their own internal processes.

A-06

Backup & disaster recovery

I sit down with clients to build a backup policy that keeps all their data safe, then walk them through what a full disaster recovery test involves: RTOs, RPOs, and the steps we can take to harden the setup.

A-07

Windows Server

For clients that still need on-prem compute, I manage their file servers with Active Directory, keep machines in line with Group Policy, and use Entra Connect to tie on-prem AD into Entra ID.

A-08

AI

Claude and ChatGPT are part of my everyday toolkit, from troubleshooting to vibe coding my own tools and web apps that automate repetitive tasks.
